Transforming AI Waste Heat into Freshwater

Aqua Compute is a sustainable solution that recovers waste heat from AI data centers and utilizes it to power an HDH-based desalination system for freshwater generation.

Learn More

Project Overview

AI infrastructure generates significant amounts of waste heat. Aqua Compute captures this otherwise discarded thermal energy and uses it to operate a Humidification-Dehumidification (HDH) water purification system.

The result is a low-cost, environmentally friendly method of producing freshwater while improving overall energy utilization.
